## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional, and cryptographic digital infrastructure for sensitive medical health records and immunization validation. Rejecting ephemeral consumer wellness tropes, decorative gradients, and playful micro-interactions, the aesthetic reflects high-assurance healthcare architecture, regulatory compliance (HIPAA, HL7 FHIR), and clinical precision.

### Design Principles
- **Institutional Reliability:** Interfaces are structured, balanced, and intentionally quiet, prioritizing immediate legibility over ornament.
- **Cryptographic Transparency:** Visual proof states (SHA-256 verification, OAuth2 authorization, FHIR endpoints) are explicitly documented using structured security tags and telemetry chips.
- **Clinical Ergonomics:** Information architecture respects dense medical metrics (LOINC codes, dosage volumes, ICD-10 keys) without sacrificing ergonomic touch targets or WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast ratios.
- **Style Archetype:** High-density Corporate Modern paired with Tonal Precisionâ€”distinguished by calibrated surface depths, crisp sub-pixel keylines, and neutral typography.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high diagnostic contrast and institutional gravitas. Pure medical white anchors the canvases, backed by subtle ice-blue tints to mitigate glare during prolonged provider and patient utility.
